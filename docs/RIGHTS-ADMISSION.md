## Original art and fictional brands

1. After actual authorization, the eligible creator records original geometric
   artwork provenance: author/date, procedural specification/version, all inputs
   and exact output hashes. Use geometric art only; no copied label artwork,
   logos, recognizable trade dress or official emblems. Originality does not
   waive individual input rights checks.
2. Use original fictional syllable brands. An appointed operator documents manual
   recognizable-brand screening and a salted known-brand blocklist check before
   artwork admission. No brand strings or screening outcomes are authored here.
3. Record candidate commitment, reviewer/date, comparison-source provenance,
   blocklist version/digest, salt-custody reference, comparison policy/version,
   result and unresolved recognizable similarity. A salted list is not a trademark
   clearance, and the manual step remains required. Do not generate keys or collect
   credentials to implement this future screening.
4. Hold ambiguous candidates for human disposition. Reject or replace unapproved
   material, preserve prior records and recheck changed bytes. No recognizable-brand
   check can imply legal identity, trademark advice or agency approval.
