## Audit procedure to commission later

Cover W-TEXT, W-HEAD, A-FORMAT, A-MATCH, N-MATCH, B-MATCH and C-MATCH for each pilot.
Use only ratified finite policies; no legal classification, physical size/boldness
or agency approval claim. Check actual final pixels, not scene-layout rectangles
or OCR agreement. Preserve half-open source-coordinate region conventions and
transforms; audit glyph unions/polygons after clipping, tilt, resizing, blur,
compression and glare. Absent evidence has no invented box. Default F1 missing
evidence remains review; do not infer visual absence from OCR nonrecognition.

For the later held-out study, audit stratified at least 40 labels: at least 5 per
hidden family and at least 20 across conforming/familiar/nuisance, all seven checks
and held-out fonts. Increase the sample if extra hidden families make these minima
exceed 40. Add every ambiguity/grey-band item and all photo annotations. Roy
personally checks at least 10 labels and resolves every disagreement. Record IDs,
sampling seed/policy under custody, identities, observations and resolutions.

Any truth/geometry error requires correction and audit expansion to its entire
causal mechanism/template/font stratum before freeze. Unresolved errors block
freeze. Preserve excluded/failed attempts; do not drop difficult items. Sampled
construction-truth audit retains residual oracle risk and is not legal verification.

Final-pixel answerability: occlusion at least 50% or cap height below 8 px is
unanswerable. Fully visible adequate-density evidence is answerable subject to
ratified ambiguity; independently adjudicate intermediate/conflicting/grey-band
cases before predictions. Photos require manually audited source-to-photo polygons.
